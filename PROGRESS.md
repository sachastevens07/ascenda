Accounts without `age_confirmed_at` (made before C2, or via the API) are asked once at the start of the quiz; "No" offers "Delete my account" (RPC `delete_own_account()`, cascades to profile, sessions, sets, swaps) or Go back. Level names: 1 Corner, 2 Dumbbell area, 3 Machines, 4 Whole gym (match exercise zones). The suggested level is highlighted ("Suggested for you") but she must tap to choose. Bodyweight: kg, 30–300, rounded to 0.1, optional (Skip), copy says never BMI or a health score; not used anywhere yet (relative-strength stats come later).
- **Level fills and Quiet Mode** (C3, 21 Oct): a slot is filled by the equipment-matched exercise if `zone` ≤ her cap, else the slot's own dumbbells then home variant if that one fits, else a same-muscle library pick (same ranking as swaps). Last resort: keep the original so the slot isn't empty. Quiet Mode = the whole session in zone 1 (Corner), the lowest zone she's always comfortable in; stored on `profiles.quiet_mode`, default false so existing testers aren't switched to corner-only sessions. At Level 1, on and off look the same. The toggle lives on the Today card and is **disabled** (not hidden) while a session is in progress, with copy "Finish this session to change Quiet Mode." Programme screen uses her level but ignores Quiet Mode. Level fills are saved as session swaps on Start; "Instead of …" is only shown when she swapped away from an exercise she could still do (not when we quietly replaced a barbell). **Custom track/thumb**, never native `Switch`.

Cycle: `PillDropdown` under the logger header; "No note" clears the phase. Swap: one `SwapSheet` per exercise row, opened by the swap icon (icon fills plum while open). Just today / Every time / Back stay as pills inside that menu. Target gap above, between and below pills is 8 px (`OPTION_GAP`).
- **Level-up moments** (C5, 21 Oct): 4 finished sessions at the current level (counted after the latest `confidence_history.created_at` or resolved challenge) → one next-zone exercise matching her equipment, preferring a muscle already on the next day. **Added as an extra slot**, never a replacement (friendlier, and it doesn't fight swaps). 3 sets × 8–12. Completing it = a finished session with at least one ticked set of that exercise. After two, offer a level-up; declining the try or the level-up waits 4 more sessions. No guilt copy. Quiet Mode does not put a next-zone lift in the session. Going back down is Profile → Your level (and Change answers still works; both write history). Own tables so a missing migration doesn't break profile load. C7 (Pro-only level-ups) is later — C5 is visible to everyone for now.
