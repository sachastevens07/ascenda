## Description (2821/4000)

```
Ascenda helps women feel at home in the gym.

Whether it's your first time or you're coming back after a break, Ascenda gives you a clear plan for every week, a guide for every exercise and a simple way to see yourself get stronger.

A PLAN THAT'S MADE FOR YOU
Answer a few quick questions and we'll recommend a routine that suits your goals, experience and schedule. Every routine has a clear finish line, from Strong Foundations in 6 Weeks to The Climb in 8 Weeks, with phases, checkpoints and a coach note for every day.

GUIDES FOR EVERY EXERCISE
Not sure where the leg press is or how to set up the cable machine? Every exercise has a first-time guide: where to find it, how to set it up, how it should feel and what to do if it's taken.

LOG YOUR SESSION IN SECONDS
Your weights from last time are already filled in. Tick off your sets, rest with the built-in timer and get a suggestion when it's time to go up. Swap an exercise in one tap if the gym's busy.

SEE YOURSELF GET STRONGER
Personal bests, checkpoint weeks that compare where you started with where you are now, and a few wins after every session. Your progress is always visible.

GYM UNLOCKED
Track every exercise and piece of equipment you've tried, with badges for your firsts: first machine, first cable exercise, first barbell lift and more.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
• A weekly streak where one session keeps your week going
• Rest weeks you earn, so a holiday or a busy week doesn't undo your progress
• Warm-ups, cool-downs and stretch sessions built in
• A planner that shows exactly what's coming up
• Single sessions for days you just want one workout
• Sessions for days you can't get to the gym

LEARN AS YOU GO
Thirty short, plain-English articles on gym confidence, technique, recovery and fuelling your training, with sources listed.

CALM AND ENCOURAGING
No shaming, no weight-loss pressure and no countdowns. Just a clear plan and a little encouragement on the days you need it.

SUBSCRIPTION
Ascenda is a subscription app. You can download it and get your personalised plan for free. A subscription unlocks every routine, guide, stretch and feature.
• Annual plan with a 7-day free trial, or a monthly plan
• Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm your purchase, or at the end of the free trial
• Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
• Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings
• If your subscription ends, your history and personal bests stay yours and you can export them

Ascenda is for adults aged 18 and over. It gives general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Please check with your GP before starting if you're pregnant, injured or have a health condition.

Privacy Policy: [PRIVACY_URL]
```

- No before and after images, body shots or weight-loss claims.
- Prices in screenshots or text must match App Store Connect for that country; it's safest to leave exact prices out of the description, as above.
